# K-pop-Entertainment-Industry-Financial-Performance-Market-Perception-Dashboard

# Financial Performance, Global Expansion, and Market Perception of Leading K-pop Entertainment Companies: A Comparative Data Analytics Study

# 📝 Description 
◽ Analysed 2020–2025 financial and market data of HYBE, SM Entertainment, JYP Entertainment, and YG Entertainment to compare financial performance, revenue structure, international expansion, and market perception.

◽ Built 4 interactive Power BI dashboards covering Financial Performance, Revenue Structure, Global Expansion, and Market Perception, incorporating KPIs, trend analysis, comparative charts, and slicers.

◽ Analysed KRW 10,926.30 billion cumulative revenue generated by HYBE and compared revenue growth, profitability, market capitalization, and revenue composition across the four companies.

◽ Integrated non-financial indicators including overseas revenue, international tours, Billboard entries, Google Trends, Spotify listeners, and social-media engagement to evaluate the relationship between global popularity and financial performance.

◽ Used Excel for data preparation and calculations and Tableau for comparative visualization, converting multi-source secondary data into business-level insights and strategic recommendations.

# 𝄜 Tools:

📊Power BI-Used to create interactive dashboards and visualise KPIs, trends, comparisons, and relationships across the four K-pop entertainment companies.

❎ MS Excel-Used for data collection, cleaning, standardisation, calculations, and preparation of financial and market datasets for analysis.
